  **How the trickier cases behave:**
  - **Overdue** means an open item whose known due date is earlier than today.
    Something due today isn't overdue, and items with no due date or already

    status column still marks them `overdue`, and the summary reads "8 open
    (4 overdue)".
  - **`--due-before`** is strictly before the date you give. Items with no due
    date are left out, and the output says how many.
  - **Filters** always combine with AND. 14 tested combinations of owner,
    status and date cover this.
  - **`--owner Priya`** with two Priyas lists nothing. It shows both people with
    their owner IDs so you can rerun with the full name or `--owner-id`. Tasks
    with an ambiguous owner are never shown under either person, but you get a
    note pointing to them.
  - **`done`** accepts an ID like `12` or `#12`, or the stable uid.
    - Repeating it on a done item changes nothing and says when and how it was
